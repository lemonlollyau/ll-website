What actually gets built

Emails that show up at the right moment.

  1. WELCOMETrigger: joins your list

    They've just met you.

    Introduce the brand, the products and a reason to buy, without turning the relationship into a wall of discounts.

  2. BROWSETrigger: views a product

    They looked. They left.

    Bring the products they were eyeing back into the conversation.

  3. CARTTrigger: leaves checkout

    They got this close.

    Pick the buying journey back up when someone leaves products behind.

  4. POST-PURCHASETrigger: places an order

    The sale shouldn't be the end.

    Set expectations, back up the purchase and lay the groundwork for the next one.

  5. WINBACKTrigger: gone quiet

    It's been a while.

    Give past customers a reason to remember you exist.
