Your flows were set up once and never touched

Questions

The things people ask.

Why charge for the audit when everyone else does it free?

Because a free audit is a sales call with a spreadsheet attached, and both sides know it. Charging means we spend seven days on it instead of an afternoon, and you get a document worth keeping. It also means we're not trying to win you in a single call, which makes the whole conversation better.

And it costs you nothing if you go ahead — the $750 comes straight off your first month.

What size brand does this work for?

Roughly $30,000 to $300,000 a month. Below that the retainer is hard to justify and you're better off with a freelancer — tell us and we'll point you at one. Above that you probably need more than one person on the account, and we'll say so rather than take the money.

We're not on Shopify. Is that a problem?

No. We work across Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Squarespace, Wix, custom builds and marketplaces. If your platform is genuinely holding you back we'll tell you and we can move you — we build sites too. If it isn't, we'll leave it alone and spend the money somewhere it matters.

How long have you been going?

The studio is new. The experience isn't — twenty years across brand, paid, email and web. You'll get a free call and a paid audit before you commit to anything, so you can judge the thinking rather than take our word for it.

Am I locked into a contract?

Three months to start, because nothing in email or paid can be fairly judged in less. After that it's month to month with 30 days' notice.

Do you work with brands outside Adelaide?

Yes. We're Adelaide based and most of our clients are elsewhere. It all runs over video and shared dashboards.

What if the audit says we're already doing fine?

Then we'll say so, you'll have a document confirming it, and we won't try to sell you a retainer. It's happened. We'd rather lose a deal than take money for work that won't move the number.
