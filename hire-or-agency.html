Why a team

One person can’t be five specialists.

More skills than any one hire.

Design, ads, SEO, email and web are advertised as separate jobs, with separate salaries. A team gives you a specialist for each, instead of one person stretching.

SEEK lists graphic designers, social media managers, digital specialists and web developers as different roles.

Nothing stops when someone’s away.

Holidays, sick days and resignations don’t pause your marketing. Someone else on the team picks it up.

Australian employers averaged 16% staff turnover over a year (AHRI, December 2024).

Faster off the mark.

A team that’s done this for lots of businesses gets moving sooner than one new hire learning the ropes on their own.

New hires typically run at 30–50% of normal output for their first three months (Expert360).

The tools come with them.

Design software, SEO tools and scheduling platforms are part of how an agency works. With a hire, the licences are yours to buy.

Adobe Creative Cloud Pro for teams is about A$1,300 a year. Semrush Pro is US$139.95 a month.

No lock-in.

Priorities change. With no lock-in contract you can change direction or stop. A salary is a fixed cost that doesn’t flex with a quiet quarter.

lemonlolly’s retainer is month to month. No lock-in contract.

Skills that keep up.

Platforms change every few months. Specialists who live in them every day keep up; a generalist has to squeeze it in.

Over half of brands report a performance-marketing skills gap, and almost two-thirds outsourced work to an agency in the past year (Marketing Week, 2025).
To be fair

When hiring is the right call.

  • You need someone on site every day — events, in-store, shooting product daily.
  • There’s a full week’s worth of work in one skill, every week. A specialist hire can make sense.
  • You already have a marketing lead who can direct them and check their work.

Or do both.

Lots of businesses get the best of both: a coordinator in-house who knows the business inside out, and an agency team for the specialist work like ads, design, SEO and web.

Your person stays close to the action. The specialists do what they do best.

FAQ

Fair questions.

Isn’t this rigged in favour of agencies?

We’re an agency, so we’d understand the suspicion. That’s why every number is on this page with its source, and you can change the salary and the agency fee. If the hire comes out cheaper, the calculator says so.

Isn’t paid leave already part of the salary?

Yes, the salary keeps paying while they’re away. We count those six weeks because you’re paying 52 weeks of salary for about 46 weeks of work, while an agency team keeps going. If you’d bring in a temp to cover, it becomes real extra cash.

Why isn’t PAYG tax added?

PAYG is the income tax you withhold from your employee’s pay and send to the ATO. It comes out of their salary rather than on top of it, so adding it would count it twice. Payroll tax is different: that’s a state tax on top of wages, and you can tick it if it applies to you.

Is GST included?

No. Salaries don’t attract GST, and most businesses claim back the GST on agency fees, so we compare everything before GST.

What about ad spend?

Left out on purpose. Whether a staff member or an agency runs your ads, the money that goes to Google or Meta is the same.

Our salaries are lower than SEEK’s averages.

Change the salary to your number. Everything else recalculates from it.

What happens if I send my report?

We email you a copy and, if you ask, one of the team will look over it and reply with a few honest thoughts. We don’t sell or share your details, and you only get marketing emails if you tick that box.
