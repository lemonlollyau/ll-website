How it works

Most agency audits are built to find problems. This one’s built to tell the truth.

01

Great agencies score great.

If your agency is doing right by you, you’ll see it in black and white — and we’ll tell you to stick with them. We mean it.

02

Facts, not vibes.

Most questions are things you can check in two minutes: who owns the account, what’s on the invoice, what’s in the report. Where it’s a gut call, we say so.

03

Price alone never costs points.

Expensive isn’t bad. Unclear is. We score whether you can see what you pay for and whether it’s working — not whether it’s cheap.

04

Only what applies to you.

Don’t run ads? No ad questions. Only been with them three months? We won’t mark them down on results yet.

    • Every answer earns points based on what a good agency should be doing. The points for each answer are fixed — they’re the same for everyone.
    • “Not sure” is never a fail. It earns part marks and comes with a two-minute way to check.
    • A few gaps are too serious to average away: no access to an account you pay for, not being able to see what reached the ad platforms, the agency owning your work, or not being able to leave with your stuff. One of these caps the score at 84. Two or more cap it at 69.

    FAQ

    Fair questions.

    Isn’t this just a sales funnel for lemonlolly?

    Partly, and we’d rather say so. It’s how we meet businesses that might need us. But it only works if it’s honest: if your agency scores well, your result tells you to stay, and we won’t try to talk you out of it.

    Will my agency find out?

    No. We never contact agencies, and we never publish names or scores. The agency name field is optional.

    What happens to my answers?

    Your score shows on screen straight away. If you unlock the full breakdown, your answers and score come to the lemonlolly team so we can follow up if you asked us to. We don’t sell or share them, and you only get marketing emails if you tick that box.

    Why does ownership count for so much?

    Because it’s the one problem you can’t fix after the fact. Lose access to an ad account, a domain or a Google Business Profile and you can lose years of data, history and reviews. Almost everything else can be fixed with a conversation.

    My agency is a freelancer. Does it still work?

    Yes. Everything here applies to anyone you pay to run your marketing.

    I don’t have an agency yet. Can I use it?

    Yes — as a checklist. Every question is something to get in writing before you sign with anyone. Including us.

    We scored low. Now what?

    Start with your “Fix first” list. Most of it is a polite email to your agency, and we give you the wording. Give them a fair go at fixing it. If they won’t, that tells you what you need to know.
