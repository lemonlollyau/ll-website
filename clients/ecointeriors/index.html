How I choose a finish: start with how you’ll clean it

By Sally · 5 minute read

One of the first things I want to know about a finish is how you’ll clean it. You’re going to live with it for years, long after the photographer has packed up.

A surface that has to stay perfect will make you anxious. One that ages well lets you live.

In the Buderim House we chose zinc for the benchtops, knowing it would patina, and cork underfoot…

Then: three decisions with photos · what you could take into your own home · credits · an invitation
